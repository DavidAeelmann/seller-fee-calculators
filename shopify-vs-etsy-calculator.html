Shopify vs Etsy: Which Makes You More Money?

Choosing between Etsy and Shopify is not just a platform preference—it is a foundational financial decision for your e-commerce business. Etsy acts as a high-traffic curated marketplace that charges substantial variable commission on every single item sold. Shopify, by contrast, gives you a standalone online store with a predictable flat subscription fee and standard merchant processing.

Fee Component Etsy Marketplace Shopify Store
Monthly Subscription $0/month (standard seller account) $39/month (Basic plan, or $1/mo promo)
Listing / Creation Fees $0.20 per item listing / renewal Unlimited free product listings
Platform Commission 6.5% on item + shipping total 0% extra when using Shopify Payments
Credit Card Processing 3.0% + $0.25 per transaction 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
Customer Data Ownership No direct customer email ownership 100% full email & marketing ownership

The Break-Even Formula

At what sales volume does Shopify beat Etsy in net profit? Because Etsy takes approximately 9.5% to 10% + $0.45 per sale and Shopify Basic costs $39/mo + 2.9% + $0.30, the mathematical break-even threshold typically sits between $650 to $800 in monthly sales volume.

Once your store reliably exceeds $1,000 per month in gross sales, staying exclusively on Etsy costs you hundreds of dollars in unnecessary marketplace commissions every quarter.

Frequently Asked Questions

At what monthly revenue should I switch from Etsy to Shopify? ▼
Most sellers find the break-even threshold is between $700 and $1,000 per month in gross revenue. Above that level, Shopify's flat $39/month plan generates higher net profit than Etsy's 6.5% transaction cut.
Can I sell on both Etsy and Shopify simultaneously? ▼
Yes. Many successful merchants use Etsy as a brand discovery channel while directing repeat buyers, email subscribers, and wholesale inquiries to their independent Shopify store where they earn higher margins.
Does Shopify charge transaction fees if I use third-party payment gateways? ▼
Shopify charges 0% transaction fees when you use Shopify Payments. If you choose an external payment gateway like an independent merchant processor, Shopify charges 2.0% on Basic, 1.0% on Shopify, and 0.5% on Advanced.
Does Shopify provide built-in buyer traffic like Etsy? ▼
No. Unlike Etsy's internal search engine, Shopify is a standalone storefront. You are responsible for driving your own traffic through SEO, organic social media, email marketing, or paid advertising.
