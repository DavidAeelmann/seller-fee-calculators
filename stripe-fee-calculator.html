Stripe Pricing & Reverse Fee Formula

Stripe powers checkout flows across millions of websites. While domestic transactions are straightforward at 2.9% + $0.30, accepting international cards or multi-currency payments adds incremental percentage markups that reduce your net payout.

Domestic Cards

2.9% + $0.30 per successful card charge for standard US-issued Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover cards.

Cross-Border Cards

Cards issued outside your Stripe country incur an additional +1.5% fee (totaling 4.4% + $0.30).

Currency Conversion

If conversion is required, Stripe assesses an additional 1% currency conversion fee on top of card rates.

Reverse Calculation: How to Receive Exactly $X

When invoicing a client for $1,000, simply adding 2.9% ($29) to the invoice ($1,029) leaves you short, because Stripe will deduct 2.9% from the new $1,029 total! To receive exactly $1,000, use the mathematical reverse formula:

Invoice Amount = (Desired Net + $0.30) / (1 - 0.029)
Invoice Amount = ($1,000.30) / 0.971 = $1,030.18

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Stripe's standard fee in the United States?

Stripe's standard pricing in the US is 2.9% plus $0.30 per successful credit or debit card charge.

Does Stripe refund processing fees when I issue a customer refund?

No. Stripe retains the original transaction fee (both the percentage and fixed fee) when you refund a customer payment.

How much extra does Stripe charge for international cards?

Stripe adds a 1.5% fee for international cards, plus an additional 1% if currency conversion is required.

How can I avoid Stripe international currency conversion fees?

By connecting a Wise Business multi-currency account, you can receive foreign funds natively without suffering Stripe's automatic currency conversion markup.
