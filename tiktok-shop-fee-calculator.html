Understanding TikTok Shop Fees in 2026

TikTok Shop has evolved rapidly into one of the highest-converting social commerce channels globally. However, seller fee structures have transitioned from early subsidized promotional rates to competitive standard platform commission models.

Standard Commission

Most product categories operate on a 6.0% commission + $0.30 fixed fee per order. Certain high-margin apparel categories may reach up to 8%.

Creator Affiliate Cut

If you recruit TikTok creators to post video reviews or host LIVE streams, you set an open affiliate commission (typically between 10% and 20%).

Fulfillment by TikTok (FBT)

Using TikTok fulfillment centers introduces separate pick, pack, and warehousing storage charges comparable to Amazon Multi-Channel Fulfillment.

Sample Margin Breakdown

Assume a trending beauty roller priced at $30.00 with free shipping for the buyer. Your product sourcing cost is $6.00 and packaging/postage costs $4.50. You offer a 15% affiliate commission to TikTok creators:

  • TikTok Platform Cut (6% + $0.30): $1.80 + $0.30 = $2.10
  • Affiliate Influencer Commission (15%): $4.50
  • Total Slicing Fees: $6.60 (22.0% of retail price)
  • Product & Shipping Costs: $6.00 + $4.50 = $10.50
  • Net Take-Home Seller Profit: $30.00 - $6.60 - $10.50 = $12.90 (43.0% Net Margin)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard TikTok Shop referral fee in 2026? ▼
The standard fee for US merchants is 6% plus $0.30 per completed order across the majority of retail categories.
Do I have to pay creator affiliate commissions on organic sales? ▼
No. Creator affiliate commissions only apply when an order is directly attributed to a creator's tagged showcase, video tag, or LIVE broadcast link.
How does TikTok Shop handle shipping subsidies? ▼
TikTok frequently offers buyers free shipping coupons funded directly by TikTok. In these campaigns, sellers are reimbursed according to TikTok's agreed co-pay terms.
Can I connect my Shopify store to TikTok Shop? ▼
Yes. You can synchronize your product catalog, real-time inventory, and order fulfillment directly from your Shopify admin using the official TikTok sales channel app.
