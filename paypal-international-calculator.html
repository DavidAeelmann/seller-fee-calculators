The Real Cost of International PayPal Payments

PayPal is globally recognized, but cross-border freelancers, contractors, and merchants often forfeit 8% to 10% of their gross earnings when collecting international client payments due to combined transaction fees and currency conversion markups.

Cross-Border Surcharge (1.50%)

PayPal adds an automatic 1.50% cross-border fee whenever the sender's account is registered in a country outside the receiver's jurisdiction, raising merchant fees to ~4.99%.

Hidden FX Spread (3.0% to 4.5%)

Rather than charging a transparent line-item fee for exchanging foreign currencies, PayPal builds a hefty 3% to 4.5% spread into their proprietary exchange rate versus the real mid-market benchmark.

When a European or British client pays your US PayPal account $1,000 USD converted to GBP/EUR:

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is PayPal's exchange rate different from Google or Reuters?

Google displays the mid-market rate (the real wholesale exchange rate). PayPal marks up this rate by roughly 3.0% to 4.5% as an undisclosed currency conversion profit margin.

What is PayPal's cross-border fee?

PayPal assesses an additional 1.50% surcharge for commercial transactions sent across international borders.

Can I receive foreign currency in PayPal without converting?

Yes, you can hold multiple currencies in your PayPal balance, but withdrawing those funds to your local bank account almost always triggers PayPal's mandatory conversion rate.

What is the best alternative to PayPal for international invoices?

Wise Business enables you to invoice clients in their local currency and convert at true mid-market rates with a tiny 0.4%–0.6% transparent fee, saving up to 85% compared to PayPal.
