Deconstructing Amazon FBA Fee Architecture

Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) grants instant Prime badge eligibility and world-class logistics, but success requires meticulously accounting for the multi-tiered Amazon fee structure.

1. Referral Fee (Typically 15%)

Amazon charges a category commission on every product sold. In most major categories (Home, Kitchen, Toys, Beauty), this fee is 15% with a minimum $0.30 fee.

2. FBA Fulfillment Fee

This unit fee covers picking, packing, shipping, customer service, and product returns handling. It is calculated strictly on packaged unit weight and dimensions.

The "Rule of Thirds" for Amazon Private Label

Experienced Amazon FBA brand builders often target the classic rule of thirds: 1/3 for manufacturing and landed freight, 1/3 for Amazon referral & FBA fees, and 1/3 for marketing and net profit. If your Amazon fees exceed 45% of your retail price, package redesign or tier reclassification is required.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Amazon FBA Referral Fee?

The referral fee is Amazon's sales commission for listing your product on their marketplace, generally 15% of the total selling price.

What determines my FBA fulfillment fee?

FBA fulfillment fees are determined primarily by item dimensions (Small Standard, Large Standard, or Oversize) and billable unit shipping weight.

What is the monthly Amazon Professional Seller subscription?

The Professional selling account costs $39.99 per month, allowing unlimited product listings without the $0.99 per-item fee charged to Individual accounts.

How can I avoid Amazon aged inventory surcharges?

Keep inventory turn rates healthy (under 90 days of supply) to avoid Amazon's aged inventory surcharges that apply to units stored longer than 180 days.
