How eBay Final Value Fees (FVF) Work in 2026

eBay operates on a consolidated Managed Payments system. Instead of paying PayPal separately, eBay collects a single comprehensive Final Value Fee (FVF) that covers both marketplace facilitation and payment processing fees.

Standard Rate Structure

For most standard consumer categories, eBay charges 13.25% of the total amount of the sale (item price + shipping + sales tax collected) plus a $0.30 fixed fee per order (or $0.40 for orders under $10).

Promoted Listings Standard

Promoted Listings Standard is a pay-per-sale advertising model. You choose an ad rate percentage (e.g., 2% to 10%), which is charged only when a buyer clicks your ad and purchases within 30 days.

Worked Example: Electronics vs Clothing

If you sell a camera lens for $200 with $15 shipping, in Consumer Electronics your fee is 9.0% + $0.30 = $19.65. If that exact same dollar amount was a pair of designer boots (13.25%), your eBay fee would be $28.79. Knowing your category rate prevents margin surprises.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does eBay calculate fees on buyer sales tax? ▼
Yes. eBay calculates the Final Value Fee on the entire gross transaction total, which includes the item price, shipping charged to the buyer, and applicable state sales tax collected by eBay.
How many free listings do eBay sellers receive per month? ▼
Standard individual accounts without an eBay Store subscription typically receive 250 zero-insertion-fee listings per calendar month.
What is the fee if a buyer requests a cancellation? ▼
If you approve a full cancellation or refund, eBay credits back the variable portion of your Final Value Fee, though they retain the $0.30 fixed per-order fee.
How can high-volume eBay sellers reduce their fee percentage? ▼
Subscribing to an eBay Store (Basic, Premium, or Anchor) lowers category Final Value Fee percentages and grants quarterly coupon credits for branded shipping supplies.
