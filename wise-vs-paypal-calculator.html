Wise vs PayPal: The Truth About Currency Exchange Fees

When sending or receiving money across borders, high-street banks and platforms like PayPal frequently promote "low upfront fees" or "0% commission" while concealing substantial fees inside inflated exchange rate spreads.

How PayPal Charges

PayPal deducts a 4.4%–4.99% cross-border processing fee followed by a 3.0%–4.5% currency exchange mark-down against the wholesale mid-market rate.

How Wise Charges

Wise uses the exact real mid-market exchange rate (the one you see on Google) and charges a transparent fee starting at just 0.35% to 0.5%.

Side-by-Side Savings Benchmark

On an annual international invoicing volume of $50,000, processing through PayPal costs approximately $4,400+ in fees and hidden spreads. Processing through Wise Business costs roughly $225 in total fees—putting over $4,000 back into your business.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Wise so much cheaper than PayPal for cross-border transfers? ▼
Wise built a proprietary network of local bank accounts around the world. When you send money internationally, the money rarely crosses borders; it moves from local account to local account at the mid-market rate.
Does Wise provide local bank account details? ▼
Yes. With a Wise Business account, you get local bank account numbers, sort codes, and IBANs in USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, and more.
Can my clients pay me on Wise if they don't have a Wise account? ▼
Yes. Clients can pay via standard local bank transfer, ACH, wire, or credit card directly into your local Wise account details just like any domestic vendor.
Is Wise safe for large business transactions? ▼
Yes. Wise is heavily regulated by financial authorities worldwide, including FinCEN in the United States and the FCA in the United Kingdom.
