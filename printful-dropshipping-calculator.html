How to Price Print-on-Demand Products for Profit

Print-on-demand (POD) eliminates upfront bulk inventory risks, but operating margins can quickly erode if you fail to account for the triangular fee stack: base blank garment production, fulfillment shipping, and marketplace transaction deductions.

Printful Base Pricing

Base prices vary based on garment quality (e.g. Gildan 64000 vs Bella+Canvas 3001) and print placement (front, back, sleeve, or inside label prints).

Platform Multiplier

Selling POD via Etsy takes ~10% off top-line revenue, whereas hosting on your own Shopify storefront cuts variable deductions to ~3%, preserving vital margin.

Pricing Strategy Formula

To maintain a healthy 30% net profit margin after advertising and platform fees, utilize the targeted retail markup formula:

Minimum Retail Price = (Printful Base + Shipping + Target Profit) / (1 - Platform Fee % - Target Ad Margin %)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Printful dropshipping profitable in 2026? ▼
Yes, provided you target retail prices between $28 and $36 for custom graphic tees and hoodies above $55. Attempting to sell $18 T-shirts leaves zero margin after production and marketing.
Does Printful charge a monthly subscription fee? ▼
Printful is free to connect with zero recurring subscription fees. You only pay when an end customer places an order that triggers manufacturing and fulfillment.
Should I charge separate shipping or offer Free Shipping? ▼
Testing consistently indicates that folding the $4.79 shipping cost into the product retail price (e.g. $32 with Free Shipping instead of $27 + $5 shipping) yields higher checkout completion rates.
Why do POD sellers move from Etsy to Shopify? ▼
Because POD margins are already compressed by base manufacturing costs, eliminating Etsy's 6.5% transaction cut by running on Shopify significantly increases net profitability per order.
