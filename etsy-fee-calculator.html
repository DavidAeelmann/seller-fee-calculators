How Etsy Seller Fees Work in 2026

Selling on Etsy involves multiple layers of direct and indirect selling charges. When you list and sell a handmade, vintage, or craft supply product, Etsy deducts fees from your total buyer transaction balance before depositing funds into your seller bank account.

1. Listing Fee ($0.20 USD)

A flat $0.20 fee is charged whenever you publish a listing or when an item sells and automatically renews. Listings expire after 4 months if unsold.

2. Transaction Fee (6.5%)

Etsy takes a 6.5% fee on the total charge to the customer, including both the product retail price and any shipping and gift wrapping charges.

3. Payment Processing (3% + $0.25)

For US sellers using Etsy Payments, Etsy charges 3% of the gross sale total plus a flat $0.25 fixed order processing fee.

4. Offsite Ads (12% to 15%)

If Etsy advertises your item on Google, Facebook, or Pinterest and a sale occurs within 30 days, you pay an additional 15% (optional for stores under $10,000/yr) or 12% mandatory (stores over $10,000/yr).

Real-World Worked Example

Suppose you sell a custom leather wallet for $50.00 and charge $5.00 for domestic shipping. Your production materials cost is $15.00, and actual shipping label is $4.50. Let's calculate your fees without offsite ads:

  • Gross Revenue: $50.00 + $5.00 = $55.00
  • Listing Fee: $0.20
  • Transaction Fee (6.5% of $55.00): $3.58
  • Etsy Payment Processing (3% of $55.00 + $0.25): $1.90
  • Total Etsy Fees: $0.20 + $3.58 + $1.90 = $5.68 (10.33% of revenue)
  • Cost of Goods & Postage: $15.00 + $4.50 = $19.50
  • Your Final Net Profit: $55.00 - $5.68 - $19.50 = $29.82 (54.2% Net Margin)

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Etsy charge transaction fees on the shipping cost?

Yes. Etsy charges the 6.5% transaction fee on the full order total paid by the customer, which explicitly includes the shipping fee charged and any gift wrapping options.

Can I opt out of Etsy Offsite Ads?

If your Etsy shop has earned less than $10,000 USD in any consecutive 12-month period, you can choose to opt out in your shop settings. However, once your shop surpasses $10,000 in trailing 12-month revenue, participation is mandatory for the lifetime of your shop at a discounted 12% fee.

When does Etsy charge the $0.20 listing fee?

The $0.20 listing fee is billed immediately when you create or renew a listing. If you list an item with a quantity of 5, you are billed $0.20 initially, and another $0.20 each time one sells and auto-renews until inventory reaches zero.

Why should Etsy sellers consider opening an independent Shopify store?

Etsy fees consume roughly 10% to 25% of top-line revenue per transaction. By launching an independent Shopify storefront, you pay a flat monthly plan plus standard 2.9% + $0.30 payment processing, allowing high-volume sellers to retain up to 80% more profit.
